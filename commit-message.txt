Normal-mode <leader>ttn only prefilled `:TruthTable ` and waited for an
argument, so an expression already sitting on the line had to be visually
selected first; pressing Enter on the prefill produced E471.

  mapping does for a selection; on a blank line it prefills as before.
- Cover both branches in spec/integration.lua: the callback's return on a
  blank line, and a fed keystroke on `A and B` producing the table.
